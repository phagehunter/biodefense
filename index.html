Reading the request. The FY2026 request proposes $3.25B less than FY2025 enacted funding. 04Sources and method

Where the numbers come from.

All figures are from the Biodefense Budget Breakdown maintained by the Janne E. Nolan Center at the Council on Strategic Risks, which compiles program-level biodefense funding from congressional budget justifications and agency budget documents. Cite them, not this page: Chwalek, Sabrina. "US Biodefense Budget Breakdown: Fiscal Year 2026 Update." Council on Strategic Risks, 9 December 2025.

What the columns mean

Actual (A) is money spent in that fiscal year, as reported after the fact. Enacted (E) is what Congress appropriated; FY2025 was funded by a full-year continuing resolution, which CSR marks “CR+”. Request (R) is the President’s budget proposal, which Congress may change.

Dollars are nominal, not adjusted for inflation. CSR notes that some lines bundle non-biodefense work, so these totals overstate biodefense-specific spending where finer data is unavailable.

What this page changes

CSR's workbook has one sheet per department. fetch_data.py reads them into one file and merges lines that share a name and never overlap in time, which is how a reorganization appears in the data; those lines carry a “moved” note instead of looking like a cut. 12 lines were merged this way. Lines that share a name but overlap in time are kept apart and labeled with their office or spending type.

Data converted 14 October 2026; CSR's dataset was last updated 17 November 2025 with December 2025 corrections.
